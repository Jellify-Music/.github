We're a volunteer group of developers dedicated to making listening to your own music library on the [Jellyfin Media Server](https://jellyfin.org) as good as it is on the big streaming services. Learn more at **[jellify.app](https://jellify.app)**.

## Jellify

**[Jellify](https://github.com/Jellify-Music/App)** is our flagship app: a cross-platform music player for iOS and Android, built with React Native.

- Artwork-forward interface that feels instantly familiar
- Discovery through similar artists and Jellyfin Instant Mixes
- Offline playback, gapless playback, and playlist management
- CarPlay, Android Auto, and Google Cast support
- Over-the-air updates so fixes reach you without waiting on app store review

## Libraries

Building Jellify means building pieces that are useful on their own, so we publish them for others to use.

| Library | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [react-native-reanimated-slider](https://github.com/Jellify-Music/react-native-reanimated-slider) | A performant, customizable slider for React Native, powered by Gesture Handler and Reanimated |
| [AutoEq-TS-SDK](https://github.com/Jellify-Music/AutoEq-TS-SDK) | [AutoEq](https://github.com/jaakkopasanen/AutoEq) headphone correction profiles for TypeScript, with device name matching and graphic EQ fitting |
| [AudioMuse-AI-Jellyfin-SDK](https://github.com/Jellify-Music/AudioMuse-AI-Jellyfin-SDK) | A typed client for [AudioMuse AI](https://github.com/NeptuneHub/AudioMuse-AI)'s Jellyfin plugin, built on `@jellyfin/sdk` |

## Infrastructure

The services behind the app are open source too: the [website](https://github.com/Jellify-Music/Website), the [API gateway](https://github.com/Jellify-Music/API-Gateway) at `api.jellify.app`, our [over-the-air update bundles](https://github.com/Jellify-Music/App-Bundles), our [Nomad jobs](https://github.com/Jellify-Music/Nomad-Jobs), and our [Discord bot](https://github.com/Jellify-Music/Discord-Bot).
